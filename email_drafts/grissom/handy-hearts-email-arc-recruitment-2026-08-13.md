# Want to read Handy Hearts before everyone else?

I am looking for 20 to 25 ARC readers for *Handy Hearts* — and I
wanted you to hear about it first.

You have been on this list long enough that I trust you. So here is
the deal before I open it to social media:

**What you get:** The full book, delivered digitally, before the
September 8 launch date.

**What I ask:** An honest review posted to at least one platform —
Amazon, Goodreads, BookTok, Bookstagram, your blog, wherever you
live online — by launch day. That is it. I do not need you to love
it. I need you to read it and tell people what you actually thought.

**What the book is:**

Don Rourke is 42, ex-Navy Seabee, blond going silver, and not a man
who talks about himself. He came to Cedar Hollow to gut a farmhouse
and get some space. He did not plan on the widow next door.

Dana Whitfield has been grieving for fourteen months. She still wears
her wedding ring. She makes bad lumber puns and feels guilty for
laughing. She is not looking to be saved.

Cedar Hollow, Tennessee has opinions about both of them.

*Handy Hearts* is a slow burn. Real grief. Small town. No
melodrama — just two careful people figuring out how to be in the
same place at the same time.

"Grief doesn't end. It changes shape."
