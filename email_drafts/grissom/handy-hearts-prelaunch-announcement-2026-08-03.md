# You're About to Meet Cedar Hollow

Hey friend —

I've been quiet for a bit. That's because I've been finishing something.

**HANDY HEARTS** launches September 8th. It's Book One of the Cedar Hollow Series, and it's the book I've wanted to write for about five years.

Here's the setup:

Don Rourke is forty-two, ex-Navy Seabee, twenty years in the trades. He came to Cedar Hollow to disappear. No one was waiting on him back in Tulsa, and he was fine with that.

Then a widow's porch collapsed on a Tuesday afternoon.

Dana Whitfield still wears her wedding ring. Two years and three months, and the flannel hanging in the mudroom still smells like her husband. She isn't ready. She isn't looking. She isn't allowed.

But Don keeps showing up.

I wrote this book for anyone who's ever loved someone they thought they'd only get one shot at loving. For anyone who thinks grief and joy can't share a kitchen table. For anyone who wanted a small-town romance that took the *grief* part seriously.

Cedar Hollow is a whole world. Nancy at the Hollow Bean has her own story starting. So do Cal and Ellen. This is Book One of at least three, and the ensemble already knows where they're going.
