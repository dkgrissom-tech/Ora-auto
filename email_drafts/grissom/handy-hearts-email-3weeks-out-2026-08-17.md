# Three weeks until Handy Hearts

Three weeks from today, Don Rourke and Dana Whitfield arrive.

I wanted to write you a quick note — not to sell you anything, just to
tell you where things stand.

The book is done. It is in good shape. ARC readers are reading it now
and I am watching the first reactions come in, which is the particular
kind of terrifying that only authors understand.

What I can tell you:

The porch scene in Chapter 3 is doing what I hoped it would do.
Dana's grief is real and present without swallowing the story.
Don Rourke says very little for about the first hundred pages and
then says exactly the right thing at exactly the right moment, and
I have read that scene forty times and it still gets me.

Cedar Hollow feels like a place. I think you will want to stay.

If you have already preordered — thank you. That matters more than
you know, especially for an indie launch. If you have not yet, the
link is below and I would be grateful.

If you know someone who reads slow-burn small-town romance and handles
grief with some grace — this might be their book. I would appreciate
the word.

Three weeks. See you in Cedar Hollow.

— D.K. Grissom
