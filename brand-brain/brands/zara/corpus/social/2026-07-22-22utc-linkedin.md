The feature that changes how you use Ora after week one: Ask Ora.

After any meeting, tap the brain icon and ask:
— "What did the client push back on last month?"
— "What did we promise in the kickoff?"
— "Which action items are still open from Q1?"

Ora answers by searching across every meeting you've ever recorded. Cross-meeting memory. On your iPhone. Local-first.

Every other tool gives you a transcript per meeting. Ora gives you a memory across all of them.

Live on the App Store this week: https://meetora-app.pplx.app
