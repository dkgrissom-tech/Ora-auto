## One change from the version you pasted back

Your reformat folded the exclusion list into the prompt, which put words like
`Appalachian peaks` and `no mountains` **inside** the text going to the model. Diffusion
models weight nouns and routinely drop the negation, so "no mountains" is one of the more
reliable ways to *get* mountains. Every geography exclusion is now handled by a positive
assertion instead — `the horizon stays low and rolling` — which the model can actually
execute. The remaining exclusions are genuine failure modes worth naming, and they sit in
a separate line you can paste into a negative-prompt field or ignore.

If your tool has no negative-prompt box, paste only the first block. It is self-sufficient.
