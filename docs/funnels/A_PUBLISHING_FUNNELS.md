- **Handy Hearts sale route:** `https://cedarhollow.pplx.app/preorder` is the one preorder page. Keep every romance CTA pointed there rather than scattering store links.
- **Coloring capture route:** `https://grissompress.pplx.app/free-printable` delivers the printable after email confirmation, tags `GP-PRINTABLE`, and redirects to `https://grissompress.pplx.app/thank-you-printable`. The delivery link is `https://grissompress.pplx.app/free-printable/download`.

- **Never auto-schedule X.** Do not create an X intake draft.
- Cedar Hollow is Muskogee County, Oklahoma: red clay, oak and hickory, low rolling land, farm ponds, gravel roads, and wide sky. Visuals show no identifiable faces and no baked-in text. Overlay copy is added in Canva only.
