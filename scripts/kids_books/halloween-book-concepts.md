# Halloween Kids Page-Turner — 3 Books for Tuesday Launch

Age target: 3-5 (early learner, 10 words/page)
Style: Soft watercolor, warm palette, cute-not-scary, Halloween-adjacent
Length: 20 pages each

---

## Book 1: "Pip the Pumpkin's First Halloween"

**Hook:** Tiny pumpkin worried he's too small to be scary — discovers he's just right for smiling.

**Emotional arc:** Fear of not being enough → discovery that being "you" is the point.

**Character design:** Round little pumpkin (Pip) with soft green vines like arms. Big shy eyes. Halfway through, Pip finds a crooked smile carved by a kind child.

**Sample opening pages:**

- Page 1: `Pip was a pumpkin, small and round. The smallest pumpkin in the patch he found.`
- Page 2: `The big pumpkins boomed, "You're too small to scare! Halloween's for pumpkins with fright to spare!"`
- Page 3: `Pip hid in the leaves. He felt so sad. Being small was the only shape he had.`

**Back cover blurb:**
> Pip is the smallest pumpkin in the patch, and the big ones say he'll never be scary enough for Halloween. But when a little girl finds Pip and carves him a crooked smile, Pip learns that the best jack-o'-lanterns don't need to be scary at all — they just need to be themselves. A cozy Halloween story about self-acceptance for the smallest reader in your patch.

**KDP keywords:** pumpkin book for kids, halloween picture book, self esteem story, cute halloween, jack o lantern story

## Book 2: "The Ghost Who Forgot How to Boo"

**Hook:** Friendly little ghost with amnesia — remembers boo isn't scary, boo is hello.

**Emotional arc:** Confusion about identity → reframing what a word means.

**Character design:** Soft cloud-shaped ghost (Bibi) with rosy cheeks. Wanders through a friendly haunted house asking every creature what "boo" means. Each animal gives a warm answer.

**Sample opening pages:**

- Page 1: `Bibi the ghost woke up one night. "Oh no!" she said. "I've lost my fright!"`
- Page 2: `"What does BOO even mean?" she cried. She floated out to find a guide.`
- Page 3: `A friendly bat said, "Boo means hi! It's how we greet the moonlit sky."`

**Back cover blurb:**
> Bibi is a little ghost who woke up one Halloween night having forgotten what "boo" means. As she floats through the haunted house asking every friendly creature for help, she discovers that "boo" isn't a word for scaring — it's a word for saying hello. A gentle Halloween tale about kindness, memory, and how the same word can mean many warm things.

**KDP keywords:** friendly ghost book, halloween kindness story, boo book for kids, cute ghost picture book, gentle halloween

---

## Book 3: "Midnight the Cat Wants Company"

**Hook:** Black cat home alone on Halloween — invites all the "spooky" creatures over and they turn out to be lovely.

**Emotional arc:** Loneliness → community from unexpected friends.

**Character design:** Sleek black cat (Midnight) with a green collar bell. Hosts a "Not-So-Scary Party." Guests: shy spider who knits, polite bat who brings cookies, a chatty pumpkin (nice callback if Pip fans read both).

**Sample opening pages:**

- Page 1: `Midnight the cat had no one to play. Halloween night felt long that way.`
- Page 2: `She lit up a candle, warm and bright. And invited the whole neighborhood tonight.`
- Page 3: `A spider knocked first with a knitted sweater. "I made this for you — it will keep you better."`

**Back cover blurb:**
> Midnight the cat is spending Halloween all alone, so she lights a candle in her window and invites the whole neighborhood in. When a knitting spider, a cookie-baking bat, and a chatty pumpkin arrive at her door, Midnight discovers that the creatures she thought were spooky make the very best friends. A cozy story about how kindness turns strangers into family.

**KDP keywords:** black cat halloween book, friendship picture book, kids halloween party, cute halloween story, inclusion book for kids

## Cross-series notes

All three books:
- Share the same watercolor style + warm palette (orange, cream, deep blue, soft purple)
- Can reference each other in back matter ("You'll love Pip the Pumpkin, too!")
- Bundle-friendly as "Cozy Halloween Trio" on Gumroad ($9.99 for all 3 digital editions vs $4.99 each)
- Each hits a different Halloween anxiety: not-enough (Pip), forgetfulness (Bibi), loneliness (Midnight)

## Production order (chronological)

1. **Saturday afternoon (today):** Feed all 3 concepts into `page-turner-generator.ts`. Generate stories with Claude in parallel (~3 min total).
2. **Saturday evening:** Kick off illustration jobs. 3 books × 21 images = 63 illustrations. At 25 sec/image via fal.ai Flux Pro in batches of 3 = ~9 minutes wall time. Cost: $3.15.
3. **Sunday AM:** Review illustrations. Regenerate any that came out scary/off-brand. Budget 10 regens = $0.50.
4. **Sunday PM:** Assemble videos + KDP interiors. ~15 min per book.
5. **Sunday evening:** Upload to KDP (interior PDF + wrap cover). Amazon review typically 12-24 hours.
6. **Monday:** KDP goes live. Upload videos to Buffer queue for @grissomshop TikTok + Instagram.
7. **Tuesday morning:** First posts fire automatically. Halloween ramp begins.
