Target TikTok account: **@toolstack-y4g**

## Status

**STAGED / DORMANT.** This brand is not yet registered with the n8n
short-form or video pipelines. The pipelines' `BRANDS` list is
hardcoded to `['ora', 'grissom', 'familybook']` in both
`n8n_workflows/workflow_short_form_pipeline_v3_postiz.json` and
`scripts/run_scheduler.py`. Adding `toolstack` there is a separate,
deliberate action.

Nothing in this folder should publish until:

3. `toolstack` is added to the video pipeline's BRANDS list.
4. `publish: false` flags in each post file are removed.

## Goal

Grow `@toolstack-y4g` past **1,000 followers** to qualify for the
TikTok Shop affiliate self-apply threshold, using entertaining
talking-animal short-form video.

## Content format

- 9:16 vertical, 18–28 seconds
- Burned-in captions
- One recurring character per post
- Payoff or twist ending
- Series-based follow CTA

## Recurring series

- **Milo the Office Cat** — workplace comedy, corporate satire
- **Rita the Raccoon** — chaotic revenge, package theft, schemes
- **Detective Barkley** — noir mysteries with silly payoffs
- **Hamster CEO** — startup satire

## Cadence (when activated)

- 2 posts per day (morning + evening)
- 30-day audience-growth sprint before any product/affiliate content

## What this brand explicitly does NOT do (yet)

- No affiliate links
- No product pitches
- No routing to `@amgriss1` (that account stays with Grissom)
- No modification of existing Grissom/Ora/Familybook flows
