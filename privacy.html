Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Don Grissom ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Grissom Press website at dkgrissom-tech.github.io/Ora-auto and the "Ora Auto Poster" application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

The App is a personal publishing tool. It collects only the information required to publish pre-authored content on behalf of the account owner who authorizes it:

  • OAuth access tokens issued by connected social media platforms (TikTok, Meta, Pinterest, Bluesky, and others). These tokens are stored as encrypted repository secrets and are used solely to publish content on the authorized account.
  • Basic account identifiers returned by the OAuth flow (for example, TikTok Open ID, Meta user ID, Pinterest board ID). These are used to route posts to the correct account and board.
  • Content assets — text, images, and video — that the account owner has prepared and stored within the App's repository, to be published on the account owner's own social media accounts.

The website itself does not require you to create an account, and does not collect personal information from visitors beyond standard server request logs (IP address, user agent, referrer) collected by GitHub Pages for the purpose of serving the site.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected by the App is used exclusively to:

  • Publish content that the account owner has explicitly queued for publication;
  • Refresh authentication tokens as required by platform APIs;
  • Log publishing outcomes for the account owner's own review.

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Platforms

The App publishes content to third-party platforms including TikTok, Instagram, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky, and Buffer. Once content is published, it is subject to the privacy policies and content policies of each respective platform. We recommend reviewing those policies directly.

4. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained until the account owner revokes authorization through the applicable platform, or until the tokens expire. Content assets are retained in the repository at the account owner's discretion. Publishing logs are retained for operational review by the account owner.

5. Data Security

OAuth tokens and other credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are never exposed in application logs or client-side code. Access to the App's repository is restricted to the account owner.

6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Revoke the App's authorization through the settings of any connected platform;
  • Request deletion of any stored token or content asset;
  • Contact us with questions or concerns about your data.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Grissom Press publishes picture books intended for children, but purchases and account interactions are conducted by adults.

8. International Users

The App and its infrastructure are operated in the United States. If you access the App or the connected accounts from outside the United States, please be aware that information may be processed in the United States and stored on servers located in the United States or other jurisdictions.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to Don Grissom at dkgrissom@users.noreply.github.com.
