# 3am Idea

it's 3am and your brain just handed you the idea.

you know the one. the kind that feels obvious and electric at the same time. the kind that makes you think *why didn't I think of this sooner.*

you also know what happens next.

you tell yourself you'll remember it. you won't write it down because writing it down feels like admitting you're actually awake at 3am thinking about this. you close your eyes.

it's gone by 7.

that's the whole reason Ora exists — not to make you more productive, not to optimize your workflow, just to be the thing you reach for in the dark so the idea doesn't slip back into wherever ideas go when you lose them.

thirty seconds. one voice note. back to sleep.

the idea is still there in the morning. *that* feeling is still there in the morning.

🎙️ Ora — think out loud, find it later.
