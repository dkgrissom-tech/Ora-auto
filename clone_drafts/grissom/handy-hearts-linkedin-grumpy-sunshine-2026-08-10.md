# Grumpy/Sunshine Is Not What Most People Think It Is

The trope has a reputation problem.

Grumpy/sunshine gets filed next to opposites-attract — the idea that
one person's warmth simply melts the other person's ice. That is a
fine story. It is just not the one I am interested in writing.

Don Rourke is not grumpy. He is quiet. There is a difference.
A grumpy character needs softening. A quiet character needs a reason
to speak. Don spent twenty years in the Navy building things and
watching people leave. He came to Cedar Hollow to work on a farmhouse
and get some space. He was not closed off — he was resting.

Dana Whitfield is not sunshine. She is warm, yes, but she is also
fourteen months into widowhood. She laughs at bad puns and then feels
guilty for laughing. She is sunshine with weather in it.

So what do you get when you put them together?

Not melt. Not magic. Witnessing.

Don watches Dana laugh and not apologize for it. Dana watches Don
work in silence and realizes silence can be safe. Two people who are
tired in compatible ways, figuring out how to rest in the same place
at the same time.

That, to me, is the real version of the trope.
