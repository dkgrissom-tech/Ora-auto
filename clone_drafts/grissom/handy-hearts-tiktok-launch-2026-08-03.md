# She Still Wears Her Wedding Ring. He's Not Asking Her to Take It Off.

Don Rourke came to Cedar Hollow to disappear.

Forty-two. Ex-Navy Seabee. Twenty years in the trades. No one waiting on him back in Tulsa.

Then a widow's porch collapses on a Tuesday afternoon —

— and Dana Whitfield opens the door still wearing the ring.

She isn't ready. She isn't looking. She isn't *allowed*.

But Don keeps showing up. With sawdust on his forearms and a truck at the end of her drive and a quiet way of fixing things that were never on the list.

Cedar Hollow is watching. Nancy at the Hollow Bean is *rooting.* And a storm is coming that will either take the porch down for good — or give Dana a reason to finally let go.
